Why precinct seats matter

Precinct committee members are the base of the party. Together they make up the county central committee, which elects the county party's officers and sends members to the state party.

Two seats per precinct

Each precinct elects one committeeman and one committeewoman in the June primary in even-numbered years.

Open seats can be filled now

Seats left empty after the primary can be filled by appointment. Your county chair can explain how it works in your county.

Raise your hand

Find your precinct, and if a seat is open, tap "I want to serve here." Your county and chapter leaders will follow up.
